Shapes

Lumps with uneven outlines, a lit top left and a shadow where they touch the table. Wells are pressed in, like a thumb dent.

Color

The colors come from your StarExplorer game. Each surface uses its complement as the accent: blue on the orange panel, orchid on the green rail, chartreuse on the violet table. The table is its dark violet. The clay uses the five planet colors, the probe cyan and the ferry amber. Gold is the action color. The tag colors come from fields in the board files.

Motion

Three held poses, one short burst on hover or press. It never runs by itself. It turns off when your system asks for less motion.
